# Art source

The GoLab 2026 speaker template supplies the background art and the cocktail
and tree gophers. The template is the source for the JPG and PNG art in this
folder. Ask the conference for reuse permission outside this talk.

The hiking SVG gopher in `gophers/` come from
[egonelbre/gophers](https://github.com/egonelbre/gophers). It uses CC0.
The sample images use the same assets as the previous theme.
